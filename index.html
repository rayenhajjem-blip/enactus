Our approach

Three pillars, one goal: impact

Every Enactus ENET'com project is designed to create lasting value social, environmental, and economic for the communities we support.

People

Social impact

Projects that strengthen education, inclusion, and self-reliance for vulnerable communities around Sfax.

Planet

Environmental impact

Concrete solutions to reduce waste, recover materials, and raise environmental awareness.

Prosperity

Economic impact

Micro-businesses and training programs that create lasting income for local project holders.
