Our story

A generation of engineers who build businesses to change the world.

Enactus ENET'com is the local chapter of the international Enactus network, run by students from the National School of Electronics and Telecommunications of Sfax.

Our mission

Making entrepreneurship a tool for impact, not an end in itself.

We build multidisciplinary project teams that identify a real need in a community, then design, test, and deploy a sustainable entrepreneurial solution measured, year after year, on its social, environmental, and economic impact.

Enactus operates in more than 35 countries. At ENET'com, our club brings together students from every field around a shared conviction: tomorrow's engineers must know how to build businesses for society.

Journey

Key milestones of the club

Founding - 2015

Creation of the Enactus ENET'com club

A small group of committed students launches the local chapter with its first pilot projects.

Growth - 2016

Structured into 5 departments

The club organizes itself into: projects, human ressources, events, media and marketing, and sponsoring.

Competitions

Taking part in national Enactus competitions

Our teams present their projects to a panel of professionals at the Enactus Tunisia national competition.

Entrepreneurship - 2024

Creation and development of startups

Our projects grow into startup ideas that create lasting social, environmental, and economic value.

Today

A growing network of partners and beneficiaries

The club keeps growing with new projects and partnerships every academic year.

Our three pillars

People. Planet. Prosperity.

The Enactus framework that guides the design of each of our projects.

People

Social impact

Education, inclusion, health, and empowerment for young people and women in communities around Sfax.

Planet

Environmental impact

Circular economy, waste recovery, sustainable agriculture, and environmental awareness.

Prosperity

Economic impact

Micro-entrepreneurship, professional training, and lasting income creation for project holders.

Our values

What guides every decision we make
